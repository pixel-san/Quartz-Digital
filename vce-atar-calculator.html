How this calculator works

Every VCE study score is scaled by VTAC each year to account for differences in the strength of competition between subjects. This tool uses VTAC's 2025 Scaling Report anchor points (study scores of 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50 mapped to their scaled equivalents for each subject) and interpolates between them to estimate your scaled score for any raw score.

Your scaled aggregate is then calculated as: your best English-stream scaled score, plus your next 3 best scaled scores in full, plus 10% each of your next 2 best scaled scores (5th and 6th subjects, if you have them). Any subjects beyond your best 6 don't count.

That aggregate is then converted to an ATAR using VTAC's 2025 Aggregate to ATAR table, which ranks the aggregate against the entire Victorian Year 12 population.

This is an estimate only. VTAC's real calculation uses scaled scores to 2 decimal places from the full statistical moderation model (not just the 7 published anchor points), plus a small number of additional rules (e.g. increments for extra VCE VET units, specific language adjustments). Small LOTE (language) subjects use VTAC's "All small LOTEs" adjustment shown in the report. Your real ATAR, released by VTAC, is the only official figure.
